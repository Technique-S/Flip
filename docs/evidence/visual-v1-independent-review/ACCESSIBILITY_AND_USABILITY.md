## Accessibility semantics verified

- Nodes carry `ariaLabel` `"<title>, <kind>, read-only"`; edges carry `"<source> <sourceHandle> to <target> <targetHandle>, read-only"`.
- Pin buttons have `aria-label="<component> input|output <index>, width 1"` and `data-locator` pointing at a real canonical field.
- Inspector uses a labelled `<select>` for component and pin, and a `role="status"` region for the selected pin's exact mapping and locator — a keyboard alternative to canvas interaction.
- The error state uses `role="alert"` and preserves access to Advanced.
- Handles have pointer events disabled; nodes are not draggable; reconnection and deletion are disabled.

## Usability / scope observations

- **UX-01 (MEDIUM):** the build result and "Compare circuits →" control are inside `<div hidden={visual}>` (`Composer.tsx:111`), so Visual mode cannot continue to comparison without switching to Advanced. No data is lost, but the read-only view is not a complete task path.
- At constrained widths the canvas needs a fresh "Fit circuit" after resize; the automated screenshots capture the pre-resize transform. This is a sequencing artifact of the test, not a layout break, but it is worth capturing in V7 human trials.
- The read-only view intentionally exposes no REF internals, LATCH structure, simulation, proof verdict or editing; those limits are stated in the pane.
